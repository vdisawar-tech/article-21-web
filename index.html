Our work

Trip booking & flight-change notifications for private drivers

One of our systems helps private drivers manage their schedules end to end. It parses incoming ride requests, checks them against the driver's calendar for conflicts, and confirms bookings. It also monitors flight status and automatically texts the driver when a tracked flight is delayed or cancelled, so pickups can be adjusted in real time.

The messaging in this system is operational and goes only to the driver who uses it — see our SMS Terms for details.
