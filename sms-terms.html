SMS / Messaging Terms

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Program description

Article 21 LLC ("Article 21") builds and operates agentic scheduling systems for small businesses. As part of one such system — a trip-booking and flight-change notification service for private drivers — automated operational text messages are sent to the private drivers who use the system. Messages include booking confirmations, schedule-conflict prompts, scheduling clarifications, and flight delay or cancellation alerts, so the driver can keep their schedule accurate.

This is not a marketing or promotional SMS program. We do not send advertising or promotional text messages, and these messages are not sent to the general public.

Who receives messages, and how consent is obtained

Recipients are the private drivers who operate the system. A driver opts in to receive these operational messages when they onboard to the system and by sending a scheduling message to the program number. Sending a message to the program number constitutes consent to receive operational replies related to that scheduling activity.

Message frequency

Message frequency is recurring and varies based on the driver's ride and flight activity.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier and plan. Article 21 does not charge for the messages themselves.

Opt-out and help

  • Reply STOP at any time to cancel and stop receiving messages. You will receive a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, after which no further messages will be sent unless you opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Program number(s)

Privacy

Mobile information and SMS data are handled as described in our Privacy Policy. No mobile information, SMS opt-in data, or consent is shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
