Program description

Article 21 operates an internal, operational SMS workflow. An automated scheduling assistant sends operational text messages — ride confirmations, scheduling-conflict prompts, scheduling clarifications, and flight-status alerts — to Article 21's business operator. These messages are used to schedule and coordinate rides.

This is not a marketing or promotional SMS program. We do not send advertising or promotional text messages, and the program is not used to message the general public.

How consent is obtained

The business operator opts in to receive these operational messages during setup of the scheduling assistant and by sending scheduling messages to the program number(s). Sending a message to the program number constitutes consent to receive operational replies related to that scheduling activity.

Message frequency

Message frequency is recurring and varies based on ride and scheduling activity.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply based on your mobile carrier and plan. Article 21 does not charge for the messages themselves.

Opt-out and help

  • Reply STOP at any time to cancel and stop receiving messages. You will receive a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, after which no further messages will be sent unless you opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
